References for accessing device capabilities and interacting with users from the browser.

## Media capture

get audio and take photos

- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Media_Capture_and_Streams_API
- To open the smartphone's camera roll use `<input type="file" accept="image/*" capture="camera">`

## Geolocation

Read the user's position from the browser

- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Geolocation_API
- https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/javascript/overview?hl=it

## Vibration

make the phone vibrate

- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Vibration_API

## Touch events

enable advanced touch gestures (e.g., multi-touch, force touch, etc.)

- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Touch_events

## Device orientation

access the device gyroscope/accelerometer

- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Device_orientation_events/Detecting_device_orientation
  - Tutorial: https://web.dev/articles/device-orientation?hl=it

## Notifications

allows the generation of notifications from the browser

- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Notifications_API
